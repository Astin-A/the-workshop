# THE WORKSHOP

I am Astin. 
I have worked in game development for 13 years. I am a university professor, a researcher, an activist, and the founder of [Chogan Studios](https://github.com/Chogan-Studios).

I love learning, and I want to know everything there is to know about everything. Not to collect trivia, but to map how things work. If I am able to help someone, I will.

---

### ENGINEERING & DESIGN

I build interactive systems. I design game mechanics, engine architecture, and art direction. 

My technical work involves rendering pipelines, hardware constraints, and systems performance. I write the underlying code that drives the engine and manages data flow. I also look at player behavior—how people interact with the systems I build, where they get stuck, and how they exploit the rules.

### PSYCHOLOGY & BEHAVIOR

I hold two Bachelor's degrees in Psychology and a Master's in Art and Science. My academic focus is in the behavioral sciences: social dynamics, behavioral patterns, and addiction. 

I study human behavior to understand why people do what they do. I analyze social trends and the psychological mechanics of addiction, looking for patterns to predict outcomes. I use this knowledge to design game mechanics and systems. I look at how players form habits, how social structures emerge in multiplayer environments, and how reward systems function. 

### EDUCATION & ETHICS

I am a university professor. I teach ethics, art, science, and programming. 

I also work as an activist and a researcher. Broadly, I examine the direct impact of systems and technology on the human condition. I look at how the things we build, study, and enforce shape the way people live and behave. 

---

### THE ARCHIVE

This site is where I put stuff I want to share publicly. 

It is not a curated blog or a finished product. A note on engine architecture might sit right next to an analysis of behavioral psychology or a piece of speculative fiction. I write code and I write fiction. Sometimes those overlap and sometimes they don't. 

Everything here is subject to change, revision, or deletion.
